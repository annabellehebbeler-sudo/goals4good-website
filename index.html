Welcome

Goals4Good is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit located in the Kansas City area with the mission to provide scholarships and support to youth soccer players, ensuring that every child has the opportunity to pursue their passion and develop their talents in a competitive environment.

Competitive soccer can provide valuable opportunities for young athletes, but club fees, coaching, tournaments, equipment, and travel can make participation difficult for many families.

About Us

Our Story

Founded by lifelong players Clare and Annabelle, Goals4Good aims to ensure dedicated players have access to a competitive playing environment to foster development of character and soccer skills, without financial barriers limiting their success. Clare and Annabelle are passionate about this cause because they believe every dedicated athlete should be able to participate in competitive sports and pursue their goals without financial need getting in the way of their dreams.

After playing soccer for so long, they are aware of the cost of competitive sports, and it is their goal to support youth players who have the passion for the game but not the resources needed to support their development. Through community support with donations and partnerships, our goal is for financial burdens to never be the reason a committed player has to quit.

Goals4Good members envision a future where financial barriers do not prevent talented and passionate soccer players from accessing the opportunities they deserve.

Funding Access

Scholarships

How the Application Process Works:

1

Apply

Complete the online form confirming age, geographic eligibility, soccer participation/level requirements, club recommendation, financial need requirements, and personal statements.

2

Review

Applications are reviewed by the Goals4Good board of directors based on the scholarship requirements, personal statements, financial need, and the number of scholarships available.

3

Documentation

Once your application is chosen to receive a scholarship, you will be asked to fill out a separate form providing one type of additional documentation that proves financial need such as proof of qualifying assistance or a pay stub.

4

Award

Selected recipients are contacted and scholarship funds are sent directly to the players' club.

Scholarships will be given in up to 2,500 dollar increments and rewarded to families that show need and have dedication to the game of soccer. Goals4Good considers household size, soccer-related expenses, and individual family circumstances when determining financial need.

Financial information will be treated confidentially and reviewed only by authorized scholarship reviewers. All scholarship money will be delegated directly to the players' club.

All players must receive a letter of recommendation from their club in order to be eligible for a scholarship.
